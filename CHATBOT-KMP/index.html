About DocuChat

DocuChat is a rule-based document analyzer that uses keyword matching and relevance scoring to answer your questions.

How it works:

  • Extracts keywords from your question
  • Searches documents for matching content
  • Calculates relevance scores
  • Returns the most relevant section (3-4 lines max)
  • Shows source file information

Supported File Formats:

  • Text: TXT
  • Documents: PDF, DOCX
  • Video: MP4, MKV, MOV, AVI, FLV, WEBM

Tips for best results:

  • Use specific keywords
  • Ask one question at a time
  • Provide context in your question
  • Answers are limited to 3-4 most relevant lines
